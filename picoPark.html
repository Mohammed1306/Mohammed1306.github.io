Pico Park 3D

Pico Park 3D is a 3D game inspired by the original concept of Pico Park. This project, a result of my solo efforts, aims to transpose the essence of Pico Park into a three-dimensional universe, offering a reimagined and immersive gaming experience.

Key Features

3D Version: The game features an innovative transition of the Pico Park concept into a 3D environment, exploring new dimensions of gameplay and player interaction.
Collaborative Puzzles: Retaining the core idea of collaboration from Pico Park, this 3D version offers puzzles and challenges that require close cooperation between players to progress through the game.
Variety of Levels:: Multiple levels provide unique challenges, leveraging the possibilities of 3D for puzzles based on perspective and environment manipulation.

Design and Development

I undertook the complete development of the game, from initial design to final realization, including coding, graphics, and user interface.

Development in C#

The entire project was coded using C#. Here are some of the features I implemented:
3D Perspective Mechanics: reating mechanisms to utilize depth and perspective in 3D was crucial for the game's puzzles, providing an immersive and innovative gameplay experience.
Multiplayer Interactions: I established mechanisms for smooth multiplayer interactions, promoting cooperation among players to solve puzzles and progress through levels.
Graphic Optimization: In addition to developing features, I focused on graphic optimization to ensure a smooth and visually appealing experience while maintaining gameplay fluidity.
